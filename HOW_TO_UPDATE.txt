Lakmee Muster 1.4.0 update (works from 1.2.0 or 1.3.0)
1. Close the app.
2. Extract this ZIP INTO your lakmee-muster project folder and choose "Replace files".
3. Start the app again (run.bat). No new packages are needed (the database uses Python's built-in sqlite3).
Your settings, history, templates and the new attendance.db live in %APPDATA%\LakmeeMuster and are kept.
